# Product Brief: TrailWhisper

## Mission & Problem
TrailWhisper is an offline-first, zero-screen nature trail audio companion built for hikers in mountain & forest dead-zones. It tackles the **Trailhead Paradox**: hikers staring at screens, waiting on cellular signal, and missing the live forest canopy.

## Core Audience & Use Context
- **Hiker / Naturalist:** Walking on rugged mountain trails or birding in dense sal/mango groves.
- **Lighting & Scene:** Outdoor sunlight, shade, or dusk; phone kept in pocket or held loosely in one hand.
- **Connectivity:** No cellular towers (Mountain Dead-Zone). Audio inference runs on-device using open weights.
- **Interactions:** Earphones/bone-conduction headphones first. 1-sentence whispers, procedural 528 Hz Solfeggio tones, 1-tap bingo toggles, zero typing or photo-taking on trail.

## Mode
**Operate & Experience:** A functional nature trail instrument where the UI recedes so nature leads.
